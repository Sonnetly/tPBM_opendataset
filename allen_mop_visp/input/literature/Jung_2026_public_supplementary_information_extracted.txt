Supplementary Materials and Methods1. Neuron culture and preparation in 96-well plates
The SH-SY5Y human neuroblastoma cell line was obtained from American Type Culture Collection (ATCC). Cells were inoculated onto T25 culture flasks pre-coated with BD Matrigel (BD Biosciences, San Jose, CA, USA) and maintained in high-glucose Dulbecco’s Modified Eagle’s Medium (DMEM, Merck D6429-1L). The medium was supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA), 1 mg heparin (StemCell Technologies, Vancouver, Canada), 1% B27 neural supplement (Life Technologies, Grand Island, NY, USA), and 1% penicillin/streptomycin/amphotericin-B solution (Lonza, Hopkinton, MA, USA), and was preserved in a CO₂ incubator. Media were renewed every three days until cellular confluency was attained. For 2D neuronal differentiation, 1 × 10⁴ cells per well were seeded onto Matrigel-coated black 96-well plates, cultured in DMEM differentiation medium containing high glucose, and supplemented with 1% FBS, 1 mg heparin, 1% B27 neural supplement, 1% penicillin/streptomycin/amphotericin-B, 10 μM Retinoic Acid (Sigma), and 50 ng/ml BDNF (Gibco). Fifty percent of the differentiation medium was replenished every 3 to 4 days for one week before the initiation of any PBM treatment.

2. Astrocytes culture
The immortalized human astrocytes SV40 purchased from Applied Biological Materials, Inc. were grown in the culture dish coated with 40 μg/mL collagen Type I using astrocyte proliferation media consisting of Prigrow IV (ABM Inc.), 10 ng/mL epidermal growth factor (EGF), 1% v/v penicillin-streptomycin, 2 mM L-glutamine (Gibco-BRL), and 10% (v/v) FBS (Thermo Fisher Scientific). Cells were cultured in a 37°C incubator, 5% CO2, and medium was changed every 2 days.

3. Microglia culture
The immortalized human microglia SV40 cell line was purchased from Applied Biological Materials, Inc. Cells were cultured in a T25 flask containing 3 mL of microglia proliferation media at 37°C, 5% CO2 incubator. To prepare 500 mL of microglia medium, add 50 mL of FBS (Thermo Fisher Scientific) and 5 mL of (1% v/v) of Penicillin/Streptomycin (Sigma-Aldrich) to 445 mL of Prigrow III medium (ABM Inc.). The cell culture media was changed every 2 days with fresh media.

4. Preparation of astrocytes and microglia in 96-well plates
Astrocytes and microglia were grown on a T25 flask until confluent. Cells were washed with PBS, detached using 2.5% Trypsin (Thermo Fisher Scientific), and resuspended in a fresh proliferation medium to subculture. Cells were plated on the black 96-well plate at the density of 1 × 10⁴ cells/mL. Prior to astrocyte plating, 40 μg/mL collagen Type I was coated on the 96-well plate for 30 minutes and washed with PBS. Cells were maintained in 1% (v/v) FBS for 24 hours before near-infrared treatment.

5. In vitro photobiomodulation
The in vitro PBM apparatus for administering 810-nm light to cultured neural cells was custom-designed and constructed. The light source consisted of 28 surface-mount 810-nm LEDs (Cat. No. SST-10-IRD-B90H-S810, Mouser Electronics) arranged on a stripboard. The LEDs were configured in four parallel circuits, each containing seven LEDs connected in series. A ground glass diffuser (Cat. No. DG100X100-120, Thorlabs) was used to distribute the light emitted from the LEDs evenly. The structural framework of the apparatus was fabricated using a 3D printer (Cat. No. 3DP-110F, Cubicon) with acrylonitrile butadiene styrene material. The LED circuit was powered by a DC power supply (DP712, RIGOL Technologies), with a total forward voltage of 21.0V applied across the circuit to ensure that each LED received a forward voltage of 3.0V, in accordance with the manufacturer’s instruction. Light intensity was controlled by adjusting the current supplied to the circuit. The black 96-well plate was used to ensure that the light exposure to each well was independent. The light intensity at the bottom of a well in the black 96-well plate was measured at 810 nm using a Compact Power and Energy Meter Console (PM100D, Thorlabs) and a Slim Photodiode Power Sensor (S130C, Thorlabs). Only the central 4×4 wells (16 wells) of the plate received PBM to ensure uniform light exposure, while all other wells were shielded with aluminum foil. PBM was applied for 3 hours, the same as in vivo conditions, and the 96-well plate was maintained in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO2.

5. In-vitro intracellular ATP measurement
Intracellular ATP levels were evaluated by BioTracker ATP-Red Live Cell Dye (Cat. No. SCT045, Sigma-Aldrich). Cells were rinsed in 1% PBS and exposed to 10 μM BioTracker in differentiation medium containing 1% FBS for 20 minutes at 37°C. The following three washes with 1% PBS were used to remove surplus probes, and differentiation media containing 1% FBS was introduced. Signals were identified using a fluorescent microscope (Nikon TiE, Nikon) fitted with a TRITC filter (Ex/Em=510/570 nm). The fluorescence intensity, indicative of intracellular ATP, was assessed using NIS-Elements software.

6. In-vitro extracellular ATP measurement
The conditioned media was amalgamated with the RealTime-Glo Extracellular ATP Assay Reagent (Cat. No. GA5010, Promega) and incubated for 30 minutes at ambient temperature on a white plate, according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Luminescence resulting from luciferase activity was measured using the Glomax multi-detection system (Promega, Wisconsin, USA).

7. In-vitro nitric oxide (NO) measurement
Intracellular nitric oxide was quantified with the fluorescent probe DAF-FM Diacetate (Cat. No. D23844, Thermo Fisher Scientific). Cells were rinsed in 1% PBS and then incubated with 10 μM DAF-FM DA in differentiation media containing 1% FBS for 30 minutes at 37°C. Superfluous probes were eliminated by washing the cells thrice with 1% PBS, followed by the addition of fresh differentiation media containing 1% FBS. Nitric oxide was seen using a fluorescent microscope (Nikon TiE, Nikon) at excitation/emission wavelengths of 495/515 nm, and fluorescence intensity was assessed using NIS-Elements software.

8. In-vitro toxicology assay
Lactic dehydrogenase (LDH)-based toxicology assay was performed to assess possible cytotoxicity from PBM. Following treatment, the conditioned medium was harvested and amalgamated with an LDH reaction mixture (Cat. No. 11644793001, Sigma-Aldrich) containing a catalyst and dye. After a 30-minute incubation at ambient temperature, signals were measured at 492 nm using a Synergy HT microplate reader (BioTek Instruments).
